# Grayson Mode Academic Basis

Grayson Mode is an optional above-grade-level challenge. It does not replace assigned fourth- or fifth-grade instruction, affect required progress, or issue gameplay rewards.

## Design rules

- Difficulty must come from mathematical structure and reasoning—not merely enormous numbers.
- Every generated problem must have a deterministic answer and worked explanation.
- A short prerequisite lesson appears before every problem.
- Students may exit without penalty.
- Problems rotate across domains and do not repeat a fixed daily sequence.

## Current problem families

| Family | Mathematical basis | Dragonswood implementation |
|---|---|---|
| Multi-step expressions | Arizona 5.OA; later expression work | Grouping symbols, exponents, multiplication, and subtraction |
| Whole-number division | 5.NBT and 6.NS progression | Contextual quotient and remainder interpretation |
| Fraction operations | 5.NF and 6.NS progression | Unlike denominators, simplification, and fraction reasoning |
| Decimal and percent reasoning | 5.NBT into ratios/proportions | Sale-price problems with decimal computation |
| Equations | Middle-grade Expressions & Equations | Multi-step inverse-operation reasoning |
| Composite volume | 5.MD volume progression | Subtracting a removed prism from a larger prism |
