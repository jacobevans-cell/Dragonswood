    What did you discover?

    InfiltrationWater enters soil. Cover, soil type, moisture, and time can change how much.
    Interception & storageLeaves catch rain; gardens, barrels, and ponds can hold some water temporarily.
    RunoffWater that stays on the surface can travel downhill toward a stream.
    Water qualityRunoff can carry soil and other materials it touches; the risk depends on the land.
