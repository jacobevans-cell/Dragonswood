DRAGONSWOOD v33 — EXPANDED CUSTOM LONG DIVISION

Built on top of v32, so the Live Poll and Random Student/Group Picker are included.

CUSTOM LONG DIVISION
• Dividend accepts 1–5 digits: 1 through 99,999.
• Divisor accepts 1–5 digits: 1 through 99,999.
• Divisor must be <= dividend for this board.
• Dynamic place-value board expands from 1 to 5 dividend columns.
• Product, subtraction, remainder, and bring-down rows expand dynamically.
• Multi-digit divisors are supported by the same divide → multiply → subtract → bring-down walkthrough.
• Problems with remainders remain supported.
• Large-divisor visual hints cap drawn dots at 30 and label the true group size, preventing the browser from trying to draw thousands of dots.
• Original long-division-quest.html remains untouched.
