DRAGONSWOOD v48.5 — PASS CONTROL CENTER

TEACHER SIDE
- PASS CONTROL is now the operational center.
- All active Bathroom, Snack, Out of Seat, and Emergency Office passes appear at the top.
- MARK RETURNED can be done from the central Pass Control area.
- ALL pass requests that require APPROVE / DENY are together under PASS APPROVALS.
- The lower Bathroom, Snack, and Out of Seat/Emergency Office sections are HISTORY ONLY.
- Stored Pass Archive remains available.
- Global Pass Blackout remains unchanged.

DAILY LIMITS
- Bathroom: 3 automatic passes/day; extras require approval.
- Bathroom occupancy remains ONE BOY + ONE GIRL at a time.
- Snack: 2 automatic passes/day; extras require approval.
- Out of Seat: 1 automatic pass/day; extras require approval.
- Emergency Office: 1 automatic pass/day; extras require approval.
- Out of Seat and Emergency Office retain their 5-minute timers and stored history.

NAME CHANGE
- Office Pass is now called Emergency Office Pass.
