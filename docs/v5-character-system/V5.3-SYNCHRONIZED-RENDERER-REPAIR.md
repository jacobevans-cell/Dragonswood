# V5.3 Synchronized Renderer Repair

## Outcome

V5.3 repairs the defects recorded in the four tester videos: independently
looping head/skin/hair layers, loose whole-character bobbing, flat or contaminated
color shading, incomplete long-hair recoloring, and tint spill onto equipment.

The release remains restricted to `jacobicusjax@gmail.com`. Other accounts keep
the legacy character resolver and assets.

## Rendering contract

`DWRPG.renderV5Character()` creates one canvas per visible character. It decodes
the base, skin, and hair WebPs together, validates their frame counts, and draws
all three from one `requestAnimationFrame` clock using the explicit state
timeline. A render replacement cancels the prior loop before creating the next
one.

If frame decoding is unavailable or any layer fails, the renderer shows one base
image only. It never falls back to three independently animated image elements.

## Motion contract

- Idle: four-frame restrained breath.
- Walk Left and Walk Right: six distinct frames with a five-pixel stride and no
  large whole-body swing.
- Attack/Heal, Hurt, Happy, and Celebrate: separate frame timelines and motion.
- Celebrate has eight frames and never aliases Happy.
- Every frame remains inside the 320-by-320 canvas safety edge.

## Appearance contract

- Skin: Light, Medium, or Deep.
- Hair: Dark, Brown, or Silver.
- Tinting preserves source luminance so facial and hair detail remain visible.
- Hair regions must connect to a scalp anchor. Equipment and costume colors are
  not valid hair regions.
- Long-hair families include their visible braids, curls, or locks.
- Fully enclosed Dawnscale and Eclipse helmet tiers use empty hair layers because
  no hair is visible.

## Asset inventory

- 80 class/family/tier characters.
- 640 base WebPs: static plus seven animation states per character.
- 1,280 synchronized appearance layer WebPs.
- Nine saved appearance combinations from three skin and three hair choices.

## Safety and rollback

No legacy character, class, equipment, inventory, XP, currency, or ownership data
is deleted. Per-account rollback sets `characterSystemVersion` to `legacy` and
clears only V5 selection fields. Global rollback sets `v5Config.enabled` to
`false` in `dragonswood-rpg-v56.js`.
